Refund policy

Effective October 27, 2026. NetFixLab (netfixlab.tech) is run by Jyoti Kumari Singh, an individual based in Gurugram, Haryana, India.

Short version: lessons 1 to 3 are free so you can try NetFixLab before you pay. Because the full course is a low-priced digital product that you can read straight away, all sales are final. We will always fix genuine payment problems.

1. Try before you buy

The first three lessons, including their comics, takeaways and quizzes, are free with no account needed. They show exactly how the paid lessons work. Please read them before buying.

2. No refunds for change of mind

Once your payment is complete and lessons 4 to 12 are unlocked on your account, the purchase is final. We do not offer refunds for change of mind, not using the course, or buying by accident.

3. When we will refund

We will give a full refund if:

  • you were charged more than once for the course;
  • you paid but the lessons were not unlocked, and we could not fix it within 7 days of you contacting us;
  • we close your account or stop offering the course for a reason that is not your fault; or
  • a refund is required by the consumer protection law that applies to you.

4. How to ask

Email support@netfixlab.tech from the email on your account within 30 days of the payment. Include your payment ID (shown on the confirmation page, on your profile and in the receipt from Dodo Payments) and a short description of the problem. We reply within 3 working days.

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method through Dodo Payments. Your bank may take 5 to 10 working days to show the money. Access to lessons 4 to 12 ends when a refund is made.

5. Chargebacks

If something went wrong, please contact us before disputing the charge with your bank. We will help quickly. Access to the paid lessons is suspended while a payment is disputed.
