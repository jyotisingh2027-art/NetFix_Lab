Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026. NetFixLab (netfixlab.tech) is run by Jyoti Kumari Singh, an individual based in Gurugram, Haryana, India.

This policy explains what information NetFixLab collects, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. In short: we collect only what the course needs, we never see your card details, and we never sell your data.

1. What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
Name, email address and passwordWhen you create an accountTo sign you in and keep your progress and purchase on every device. Passwords are stored only in hashed form; nobody can read them.
Lesson progress and quiz attemptsWhile you read with an accountTo show your progress, mark lessons as read and improve lessons people find hard.
Purchase record: payment ID, amount, currency, status and dateWhen you buy the full courseTo unlock the paid lessons and as proof of purchase. We do not receive your card or bank details.
Contact form messages: name, email and messageWhen you write to usTo reply to you.
Anonymous usage events: a random browser ID, the lessons you open, quiz answers (right or wrong), where you came from (for example "google" or "direct") and device type (mobile or desktop)When you use the site, with or without an accountTo understand how the course is used, for example how many visitors finish the free lessons. The random ID is not linked to your name or email unless you create an account. We do not store IP addresses in these records.

Your browser also keeps a few small items in local storage (your sign-in session, the random browser ID and progress made before signing up). Microsoft Clarity sets its own first-party cookies to recognise repeat visits (see below). We do not use advertising cookies.

2. Who handles your data for us

  • Supabase stores accounts, progress, purchase records, contact messages and usage events (servers in Singapore).
  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website, runs the code that confirms payments, and provides Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits (page, referring site, country and device type) without cookies and without tracking you across sites.
  • Dodo Payments runs the checkout as the merchant of record. It collects your payment details, billing address and email under its own privacy policy, and sends us only the payment ID, amount, status and email.
  • Microsoft Clarity records how visitors use the pages (clicks, scrolling and mouse movement) as heatmaps and session replays, so we can see which parts of the lessons are confusing. Account pages, the payment window, the contact form and anything typed into forms are hidden from these recordings. Clarity uses cookies and processes data under Microsoft's privacy statement. You can block it with any common ad or tracker blocker.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and a code library to your browser, which means they see your IP address when the page loads.

These providers process data only to deliver their service. Some are outside your country; we rely on their standard contractual and security commitments when data is transferred.

3. What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.
  • We do not see or store card numbers.
  • We do not send marketing emails unless you clearly ask for them.

4. How long we keep it

Account, progress and purchase records are kept while your account exists. Purchase records may be kept longer where tax or accounting rules require it. Contact messages are kept for up to two years. Anonymous usage events are kept for up to two years, then deleted or aggregated.

5. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to stop processing it. Write to support@netfixlab.tech from the email on your account; we reply within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your access to the course.

  • United States (including California): you have the rights above, and we will not treat you differently for using them. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA.
  • India: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may access, correct and erase your data and raise a grievance. Our grievance contact is Jyoti Kumari Singh, at support@netfixlab.tech.
  • European Economic Area and UK: we rely on performing our contract with you (accounts, progress, purchases) and our legitimate interest in improving the course (anonymous usage events). You may object, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

NetFixLab is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Learners under 18 should use the site with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), access to the database is restricted so each learner can read only their own records, and payments are confirmed with signed messages from Dodo Payments. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use elsewhere.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell signed-in learners on the site.
